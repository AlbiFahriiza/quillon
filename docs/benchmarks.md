## Reading the numbers

- Render-only fps measures the compositor (tiny-skia raster,
  linear-light blend, text shaping); it is the number comparable
  across machines and against other engines' render-only claims.
- End-to-end includes decode, composite, x264 encode, and mux via
  the system ffmpeg — expect it to dominate on all CPU paths.
- Validate cost is the agent-loop step: document in, verdict out.
- The corpus is the template gallery plus the batch acceptance
  fixture; no single synthetic scene headlines anything.
